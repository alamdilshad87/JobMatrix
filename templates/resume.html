How It Works

Step 1: Upload Files

Upload your job description and a folder of resumes in PDF, DOCX, or TXT format.

Step 2: Preprocessing

The system cleans, parses, and extracts text content from resumes and job descriptions.

Step 3: TF-IDF Vectorization

Transforms resume and job description text into numerical vectors using TF-IDF.

Step 4: Cosine Similarity

Measures how closely each resume matches the job using cosine similarity scores.

Step 5: Ranked Output

Displays the top 5 matching resumes on a clean HTML page for quick review.
